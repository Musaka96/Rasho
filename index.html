About

I sculpt and texture believable characters and creatures — from AAA production heads to personal creature studies.

I'm Rastko Blagojevic, a 3d character & texture artist based in Novi Sad, Serbia. I spent six years as a technical modeler at 3Lateral / Epic Games — scan matching, texturing and grooming on titles like Death Stranding 2, Marvel's Spider-Man 2, Horizon Forbidden West and MetaHuman Creator — and now focus on character and creature art.

I've studied creature modeling and texturing under Henning Sanden and Gael Kerchenbaum, and work across the full asset pipeline: sculpt, retopology, UVs, and PBR texturing.
