About

I build believable characters and surfaces — from a first ZBrush sculpt to a real-time MetaHuman that holds up in close-up.

I'm Rastko Blagojevic, a 3d character & texture artist focused on high-fidelity character art, PBR texturing and digital humans. I work across the full asset pipeline — sculpt, retopology, UVs, bakes, look development and real-time integration.

My toolset centers on ZBrush, Substance 3D, Maya, Marvelous Designer, Mari, MetaHuman Creator, Marmoset Toolbag and Unreal Engine 5. I care most about the details that make a surface feel real: how light enters skin, how an edge wears, how a material remembers its history.
